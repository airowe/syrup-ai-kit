# Recording guide (iPhone, real Siri audio)

## Setup

1. Turn on **Do Not Disturb**, so no banners appear.
2. Turn the media volume to about 75%. Siri's voice is captured from the system audio.
3. In Control Center, long-press **Screen Recording** and turn the **Microphone on**. That captures your spoken ask. If you'd rather type the asks, leave it off; captions will carry them instead.
4. Set Siri's voice response to **Always** (Settings → Siri → Siri Responses).
5. Record in a quiet room.

## After (the fork app, signed in): one recording per ask

1. "In Bluesky, look up the profile bsky.app and tell me how many followers it has."
2. "In Bluesky, find accounts about astronomy."
3. "Post 'hello from Siri' on Bluesky." Answer **"Yes"** out loud, and let the composer open. Don't tap Post.

Wait 2 s before speaking and 2 s after Siri finishes before you stop recording.

## Before (no tools)

Ask the same followers question with an app that has no tools. Either:

- delete the fork app and install the official Bluesky app, then ask; or
- if that's a hassle, ask "How many followers does bsky.app have on Bluesky?" with the fork removed. Siri will web search or say it can't help, and either is the honest "before".

Use whatever Siri really says. I'll caption it as is.

## Hand-off

AirDrop the 4 recordings to the Mac. They land in `~/Downloads`. Then tell me the file names.

## What's on screen

- Start every take inside Bluesky on a screen with no one else's posts: Settings, or your own profile. Siri's card is see-through, so whatever is behind it shows.
- Hold the side button to call Siri. Don't say "Hey Siri".
- For the "before" take, start on a home screen with a plain wallpaper and no widgets.
- One take per ask: start recording, wait 2 s, hold the side button, ask, let Siri finish, wait 2 s, stop.
