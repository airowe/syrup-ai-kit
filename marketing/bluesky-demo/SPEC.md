# Bluesky demo: spec (Before and after the kit)

Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, about 35–40 s. The palette, type and end card match the first cut (`SPEC-three-asks.md`).

## Sound

- **Siri's voice:** the real audio from the iPhone screen recordings. Nothing is synthesised or imitated, and there's no "Hey Siri" (cut before the wake phrase).
- **Music:** `assets/music-upbeat.mp3`. It sits at −20 dB under speech and −12 dB in the gaps, with 0.25 s ramps. It lifts on the composer reveal and fades out over 1.5 s on the end card.
- **Effects:** a click on "Yes" only. The pops are dropped, because the voice now carries each beat.
- **Loudness:** −16 LUFS integrated, peaks at −1 dBTP or lower.

## Banned

- Imitating Siri's voice or Apple's interface outside of real footage.
- Footage showing notifications, the home screen, other people's posts or the volume indicator.
- A caption that disagrees with what Siri actually says.
