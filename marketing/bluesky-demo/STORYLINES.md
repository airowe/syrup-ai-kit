# Bluesky demo: storylines

Source footage: iPhone screen recordings from the Siri evaluation runs on 2026-11-21 and 2026-11-22. They are silent, so the asks and replies are shown as text.
Usable moments: the @bsky.app follower count, the astronomy account search, the draft confirmation, and the composer opening with the draft text.
Left out: the trending reply (political headlines) and the NASA profile (a 4-follower lookalike shown for about a second).
Cropped out: the home screen, Gmail, notification banners, and the feed under each Siri card.

## A. Three asks, one app (recommended)

Each beat opens with the spoken request typed out large, and the real Siri card rises in beneath it.

- Beats: followers → accounts → draft confirmation → the composer opens full screen → end card.
- Look: the launch video's palette and type.
- Sound: the launch music bed, plus a tick as each card lands.
- About 30 s.

## B. It asks before it posts

Opens on the real Cancel/Post card, freezes on "Post", and labels it "Siri asks first". Then the composer opens, and the two read-only asks follow quickly. About 20 s.

## C. One phone, no cuts

The phone's real screen stays centred for the whole film, and the camera punches into each Siri card. The asks print above it like a transcript. It ends by pulling out from the composer to the end card. About 30 s.

## Picked on 2026-11-22: Before and after the kit (replaces A)

Feedback on the first cut: the music was wrong, it needed a story, and Siri's voice matters.

- **Before (about 6 s).** The same question asked of an app with no tools: "In Bluesky, how many followers does bsky.app have?" Whatever Siri really does, it's real footage with Siri's real voice. Title: "Most apps can't answer Siri yet."
- **The turn (about 3 s).** "Add Syrup AI Kit." One real line of the tool code, from `GetProfileIntent`.
- **After (about 20 s).** The same question gets the real answer, spoken. Then the astronomy accounts, then "Post 'hello from Siri'", the confirmation, "Yes", and the composer opening. Each step is the real recording with its sound.
- **End card.** The launch card.

Sound: the real Siri audio from the iPhone's screen recordings. An upbeat HeyGen track runs under it, ducked under speech, with a lift on the composer.
